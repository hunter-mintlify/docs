Mangoes are stone fruits from the Anacardiaceae family, prized for their sweet, juicy flesh and tropical aroma. They are among the most widely consumed fruits globally and grow primarily in tropical and subtropical regions.

## Varieties and ripeness

Hundreds of cultivars exist, including Tommy Atkins, Ataulfo, and Kent. Ripe mangoes yield slightly to gentle pressure and emit a sweet fragrance near the stem. Skin color varies by variety and is not always a reliable ripeness indicator.

## Culinary uses

Mangoes are eaten fresh, blended into smoothies, dried, or used in salsas, chutneys, and desserts. Their natural sweetness pairs well with spicy and savory flavors.
