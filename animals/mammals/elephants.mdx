title: "Elephants"
description: "A guide to elephants, the largest living land mammals."
---

Elephants are the largest land animals alive today. They are recognized by their long trunks, large ears, and ivory tusks. Two living species exist: the African elephant and the Asian elephant.

## Physical Characteristics

Adult African elephants can weigh up to 6,350 kg (14,000 lb) and stand about 3 to 4 meters (10 to 13 feet) tall at the shoulder. Their trunks contain over 40,000 muscles and are used for breathing, smelling, touching, grasping, and producing sound.

## Behavior and Society

Elephants live in matriarchal family groups. They communicate through infrasound, vibrations below the range of human hearing that can travel for kilometers.

<Note>
  Elephants demonstrate advanced problem-solving skills, tool use, and empathy, including behavior that suggests mourning their dead.
</Note>

## Conservation

Both species are threatened by habitat loss and poaching for ivory. African elephants are listed as endangered, while Asian elephants are classified as endangered by the IUCN.
