Sharks are cartilaginous fish with skeletons made of cartilage instead of bone. They first appeared more than 400 million years ago, before the earliest trees, and today include over 500 species.

## Size range

The whale shark is the largest fish alive, reaching around 12 meters (40 feet) or more. At the other end, the dwarf lanternshark is about 20 cm (8 inches) long.

## Senses

Sharks have some of the most sensitive senses in the ocean:

- **Smell:** Many species can detect tiny amounts of blood in the water.
- **Electroreception:** Organs called ampullae of Lorenzini sense the weak electric fields produced by living animals.
- **Lateral line:** Detects movement and vibrations in the surrounding water.

## Teeth

Sharks grow teeth in rows and constantly replace lost ones. Some species go through thousands of teeth in a lifetime.

<Warning>
  Many shark species are threatened by overfishing and bycatch. Sharks grow slowly and have few young, so populations recover slowly.
</Warning>
