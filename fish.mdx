Fish are aquatic vertebrates that breathe through gills and usually move with fins. They live in almost every aquatic habitat on Earth, from mountain streams to the deep ocean.

## Key traits

- **Gills:** Extract oxygen from water.
- **Fins:** Provide steering, balance, and propulsion.
- **Scales:** Protect the body in most species.
- **Cold-blooded:** Most fish match their body temperature to the surrounding water.

## Main groups

| Group | Examples | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Jawless fish | Lampreys, hagfish | No jaws or paired fins |
| Cartilaginous fish | Sharks, rays | Skeleton made of cartilage |
| Bony fish | Salmon, tuna, goldfish | The largest group, with a bony skeleton |

## Habitats

- **Freshwater:** Rivers, lakes, and ponds.
- **Saltwater:** Oceans and seas.
- **Brackish water:** Estuaries where fresh and salt water mix.
