Oranges are citrus fruits from the tree _Citrus × sinensis_. They are among the most widely grown fruits in the world, eaten fresh and squeezed into juice.

## Where oranges come from

The sweet orange is a hybrid of the pomelo and the mandarin, and it originated in Southeast Asia. Today, major producers include Brazil, the United States (Florida and California), Mexico, Spain, and China.

## Common varieties

| Variety | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| Navel | Seedless and easy to peel, with a "navel" at the blossom end |
| Valencia | Juicy and slightly tart, the classic juicing orange |
| Blood orange | Deep red flesh with a berry-like flavor |
| Cara Cara | A pink-fleshed navel orange that is sweet and low in acid |
| Seville | Bitter and sour, used for marmalade |

## Nutrition

Oranges are known for their vitamin C. One medium orange covers most of an adult's daily vitamin C needs and also provides fiber, folate, and potassium.

## How to pick a good orange

- **Heavy for its size:** heavier oranges are usually juicier.
- **Firm, smooth skin:** avoid soft spots or mold.
- **Color isn't everything:** some ripe oranges keep green patches on the peel.
