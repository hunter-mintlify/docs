Bananas are elongated, edible fruits produced by large herbaceous flowering plants in the genus *Musa*. They are one of the most widely eaten fruits in the world.

## How bananas grow

Banana plants are often mistaken for trees, but they are actually giant herbs. The "trunk" is a pseudostem made of tightly packed leaf bases. Fruit grows in hanging clusters called hands, and several hands make up a bunch.

## Common varieties

| Variety | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| Cavendish | The most common supermarket banana |
| Gros Michel | The dominant export banana before Cavendish |
| Red banana | Reddish-purple skin and a sweeter flavor |
| Plantain | Starchy cooking banana, usually eaten cooked |

## Ripeness

- **Green:** firm and starchy, less sweet.
- **Yellow:** soft and sweet, ideal for eating fresh.
- **Brown spots:** very sweet, excellent for banana bread and smoothies.
