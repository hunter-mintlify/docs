Bananas are elongated, edible fruits produced by large herbaceous flowering plants in the genus *Musa*. They are a very widely eaten fruits in the world.

## How bananas grow

Banana plants are often mistaken for trees, but they are actually giant herbs. The "trunk" is a pseudostem made of tightly packed leaf bases. ## Ripeness

Bananas keep ripening after they are picked. As they ripen, starch converts to sugar, the flesh softens, and the peel changes color.

- **Green:** firm and starchy, less sweet. Green bananas are high in resistant starch, which digests slowly. They work well cooked, boiled, or fried, and will ripen on the counter in a few days.
- **Yellow:** soft and sweet, ideal for eating fresh. Most of the starch has turned to sugar, giving a balanced flavor and creamy texture. Yellow bananas with slightly green tips are a good choice if you want them to last a few more days.
- **Brown spots:** very sweet, excellent for banana bread and smoothies. The flesh is soft and easy to mash, and the flavor is at its most intense. Fully brown bananas can be peeled and frozen for later baking.

<Tip>
  To speed up ripening, place bananas in a paper bag, optionally with an apple. Both fruits release ethylene gas, which triggers ripening. To slow it down, separate bananas from the bunch and keep them away from other fruit.
</Tip>
